# Product Scope & Feature Preservation

## In scope across the full product roadmap

The following features are part of AAP's intended long-term content. A later Phase means **deferred implementation**, not deleted product scope.

- Account/Character identity, permanent death and asset disposition.
- HP, Stamina, Mana, Hunger, Infection and time-driven survival.
- Stats, Traits, Class, levels, EXP, milestones and build choices.
- Turn-based combat, Enemy Intent, enemy AI, skills, cooldowns, Guard, Escape and Surrender.
- Province/map/location hierarchy, dungeons, bounded Runs, loot selection and return encounters.
- Items, weight-based inventory, starter bag, Scrap, crafting, Salvage, equipment, affixes, Quality, durability, repair and enhancement.
- Skills, Skill Orbs, Skill Fragments, Awakening Shards and Power Library.
- Gold, Account Bank, NPC shops/services, player Market, delivery, direct trade and Auction.
- Starter Room, homes, rent, Fridge, storage, parking, vehicles and property events.
- Companion missions, recovery locker, Robots, energy and repairs.
- Association, Simulation/Tower, player Rank, Season, Job Board, quests, Guilds, shared storage, Guild Base and competition.
- Tutorial, localization, UI navigation, offline time, Pause/Resume, Admin content control, version/updates and server-integrity policies.
- Province chat and moderation as a later social feature.

## Explicit launch exclusions

- No checkout/payment processing, paid Gacha draws, paid Gacha Tickets, cash-out, Diamond-to-Shard conversion or paid Raid Token refill.
- No farming/crops.
- No permanent License Rank system.
- No owning multiple houses as a rental business; no real-estate player market without a new decision.
- No Codes, Auto Attack, Lucky or Gamepass unless the owner explicitly reopens scope.
- No extra Character death causes beyond HP = 0, Hunger = 0 or Infection at maximum.
- No one-sided gifting/transfer at launch baseline.
- No unapproved weapon/class restriction or expanding the Class roster beyond the selected design.

## Prototype versus production

The first UI prototype may use clearly labelled fixtures to prove screen flow and game feel. It must not present mock/local state as a real persistent online game. Later phases add server-authoritative persistence, account protection, actual inventory/ownership transitions, time reconciliation and operational safeguards.

## Public exposure rule

Phase 1 and Phase 2 are private/internal test phases only. Public/shared play must not start before Phase 3, because Hunger exists before the Fridge safety loop and because identity, persistence, support, outage handling and recovery gates must be proven. Completing Phase 3 is only the earliest eligibility point; public release still requires all applicable security, privacy, asset-integrity, support, backup/recovery, UI/accessibility and operational gates to pass.
