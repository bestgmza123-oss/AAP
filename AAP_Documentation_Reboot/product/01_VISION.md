# Product Vision — AAP

## One-sentence pitch

**Anime Apocalypse (AAP)** is an English-first, UI-Driven anime-comic zombie-apocalypse survival RPG in a browser, where players prepare, enter turn-based dungeon Runs, choose what to bring home, manage survival resources, and gradually build a safer and more capable life in a world that continues while they are offline.

## Intended player fantasy

The player arrives at a capital safe zone after fleeing a zombie outbreak. They receive only a basic room and must earn food, medicine, materials and money by exploring dangerous provinces. Every expedition is a decision: what to bring, how much risk to accept, which enemy action to Guard, what loot to keep, and whether to spend resources to escape. Long-term play expands into crafting, equipment, property, transport, social relationships, companions, robots, guilds, trade and asynchronous competition.

## Core loops

1. **Expedition loop:** Prepare → choose Province/Dungeon → enter a bounded Run → turn-based combat → select loot by weight → resolve up to one return encounter → return to Province.
2. **Resource loop:** Explore → earn items/Scrap → keep, sell, salvage or craft → maintain HP/Hunger/Infection → prepare again.
3. **Long-term loop:** Gain levels → allocate Stats and skills → improve gear → reach harder areas → establish home/transport → use helpers → trade/socialize/compete as systems unlock.

## Design principles

- Preparation must matter: food, medicine, Mana, bags, equipment and skill choices affect readiness.
- Resources should have multiple useful sinks: Scrap can be crafted, salvaged or traded; it is not only vendor trash.
- Risk must be communicated before confirmation.
- Property/location and transport should create meaningful advantages.
- Permanent loss is part of the game, but its rules must be explicit and predictable.
- Decisions matter more than repetitive clicking.
- Time, ownership, RNG and outcomes must be trustworthy.
- The UI is the play space. Visual quality, usability and responsive behavior are release criteria, not final polish.

## World seed

A country has been overrun by zombies. The capital remains a Safe Zone with community and services. The player reaches the capital, receives a starting room, and must obtain food and income through expeditions. Working names are **Acheria** (country), **Lumen** (capital) and **Velra** (starting Province); all three remain placeholders until final world content is approved.

## Visual identity

Anime-comic apocalypse; dark foundations, angular panels, controlled hazard/neon accents, halftone texture, purposeful iconography, and readable content areas. Avoid making every panel glow. Art assets should be replaceable without changing rules. See `../experience/01_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md`.

## Language

English is the initial player-facing language. Localization support is a first-class requirement from the beginning. Design and handoff notes may be written in Thai.
